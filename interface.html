A smooth outline is only one criterion. The proof checks the same letters at 11 stops, with the current native axes and smoothing.

Stroke & counters

Keep ink weight steady, counters open, and outlines free of introduced crossings. These are measured below.

Family & rhythm

Judge consistent stems, bowls, terminals, baseline, spacing, and word texture together. Geometry checks cannot approve these.

How to read the proof

The experimental tolerances are 12% for ink area and 20% for a horizontal stroke estimate, relative to the weighted source measurements. The stroke estimate samples side spans at five heights; it is not a full stem or contrast model. Counter count is checked only when all contributing sources agree; a counter shrinking below 60% of the weighted source area is flagged. Native source overlaps are allowed at exact endpoints; crossings introduced inside a blend are flagged on its drawn vector outline. References use native letter constructions. Different constructions cannot be mixed. Each glyph uses one construction throughout its outline. Passing these checks does not mean a font is well designed.
